`townlight_core`. Its README, user manual and governing `AGENTS.md` describe
current source and fresh-install boundaries. Signed Windows installed acceptance
for the coordinated naming, Access and Notice candidate remains pending.

The CO-3 ledger, CO-4 report, CO-7 freeze readiness and CO-9 release/audit reports
are historical records for their identified releases. Their original filenames,
organization/package identities, commands, URLs and receipt hashes are preserved.
The historical provenance policy and release recovery status likewise describe
their dated release lines. They do not certify this renamed candidate or provide
current installation commands. The dated municipal ingestion proof under
`../qa/` remains an original proof for its own inputs.

Historical records are exempt from the current naming exception registry; that
exemption does not authorize old current package/import names or product titles.
Do not relabel a historical artifact to satisfy a current dependency or gate.
